Darl-Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Darl-Bot is a Discord bot that provides moderation, welcome messages, tickets, server templates, and other server-management features.

2. Information We Collect

Darl-Bot may process information required to provide its features, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, messages or commands used with the bot, and server configuration data.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate and improve Darl-Bot's features, including moderation, tickets, welcome systems, and server configuration.

4. Data Storage

We only retain data that is necessary for the bot's functionality. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Data Removal

Server owners may request deletion of stored server-related data. Contact the Darl-Bot support team to request data removal.

6. Third-Party Services

Darl-Bot operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Darl-Bot's features or data practices change.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data-removal requests, contact the Darl-Bot support team.
